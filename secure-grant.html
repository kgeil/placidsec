How it works

Plan. Check. Act.

Three clear stages, agreed with your town before any work begins.

11. Make a plan

Start with a clear plan

You have the funding. Now you need to know exactly what work will be done, when it will happen, and what you will get.

Review the grant
We look at what was approved and any work your town has already done.
Talk with your team
We learn how your water or sewer system runs and plan around the people and equipment that keep it working.
Put the work in writing
We agree on the tasks, schedule, and reports before the work begins.

Your town knows what to expect from the start.

22. Check the system

Find the real weak spots

We check the systems your town relies on. We agree on safe methods with your operators before assessing sensitive equipment.

Cybersecurity review
For covered drinking-water systems, we prepare or update the required cybersecurity vulnerability analysis (CVA).
Equipment list
We document computers, connected equipment, and control systems without unnecessary active scanning.
Access and safeguards
We check remote access, account permissions, passwords, MFA, network separation, and policies.
Careful testing
If testing is needed, we agree on the methods with your operators first.

You get a report that clearly explains what we found.

33. Take action

Fix what matters first

We help you decide what to tackle first and what can wait. We explain the choices in plain language.

A practical to-do list
We rank the fixes by risk, cost, and their effect on daily operations.
A plan for incidents
We write an incident response and continuity plan, then walk your team through a tabletop exercise so they know whom to call and how to keep water flowing.
Help making changes
With an implementation grant, we can help improve access, policies, monitoring, and other safeguards approved for your project.
Updates for town leaders
We explain what was done and what still needs a decision.

Larger systems may also need help with program leadership, logs, and certification.
